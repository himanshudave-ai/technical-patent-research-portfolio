## 3. Relationship Analysis

1. **Physical attachment:** Both semiconductor dies are attached to the metallic pad.
2. **Electrical connections:** The first die connects to the first set of leads; the second die connects to the second set of leads; and the two dies are electrically connected to each other.
3. **Functional relationship:** The first die controls switching of transistors in the second die.
4. **Thermal structure and electrical isolation:** The heat spreader is attached to the second die via electrically insulating material. This describes the claimed interface; it should not be confused with a direct electrical connection.
5. **Heat-spreader geometry:** The heat spreader includes an exposed pad and a protrusion extending from that pad.
6. **Encapsulation:** The mold compound covers portions of the listed package components. The claim does not say that every component is completely covered.

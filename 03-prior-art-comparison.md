## 3. Key Technical Differences

### A. Thermal Architecture

The earlier patent describes two heat sinks, with the first and second electronic chips attached to their respective heat sinks.

The target patent describes two semiconductor dies attached to a metallic pad and a heat spreader attached to the second die through electrically insulating material.

### B. Electrical Architecture

The earlier patent uses an interfacing substrate containing electrical circuitry between the two chips.

The target patent expressly describes electrical connections between the two dies and their respective lead sets.

### C. Additional Structural and Functional Limitations

Target Claim 1 additionally specifies:
- An electrically insulating material between the second die and heat spreader.
- An exposed pad and protrusion forming part of the heat spreader.
- A switching-control function performed by the first die.
- A mold compound covering portions of specified components.

These limitations are not expressly recited in the earlier patent's Claim 1.

## 4. Preliminary Assessment

The earlier patent is a potentially relevant technical reference because it describes a stacked semiconductor chip arrangement and double-sided cooling architecture.

However, the two independent claims describe different thermal attachment arrangements and electrical relationships.

Based only on the two Claim 1 texts reviewed, the earlier claim does not expressly establish every limitation of the target Claim 1.

A complete assessment requires reviewing the earlier patent's full description, drawings, dependent claims, and relevant disclosure, as well as other potentially relevant prior-art documents.

## 5. Research Limitations

- This is a preliminary claim-to-claim comparison.
- Absence of a feature from an independent claim does not establish its absence from the full patent disclosure.
- No final novelty, validity, or infringement conclusion is made.
- Any final assessment requires source-specific evidence and applicable patent-law analysis.

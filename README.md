## Research Methodology

1. Identify the relevant patent and define the analysis scope.
2. Break the selected claim into individual technical elements.
3. Record the stated connections, structural relationships, and functional limitations.
4. Map each element to supporting passages in the source patent.
5. For prior-art comparisons, review relevant claims, description, figures, and publication details.
6. Clearly distinguish disclosed features, partial matches, and unresolved questions.

## Important Notes

- These projects are independent technical learning samples based on publicly accessible patent documents.
- A matching technical term alone does not establish that every claim limitation is disclosed.
- Prior-art conclusions require source-specific evidence and careful review of each claim element and relationship.
- This portfolio is for technical research and educational purposes only; it is not a legal opinion or a patentability determination.

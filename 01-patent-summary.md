## 2. Technical Problem

Semiconductor devices generate heat during operation. Effective heat dissipation is important for maintaining device performance and reliability. At the same time, electrical isolation between the semiconductor die and the heat spreader may be required.

## 3. Proposed Technical Solution

The patent describes a semiconductor package using a heat spreader and electrically insulating material. The arrangement is intended to support thermal management while maintaining electrical isolation.
